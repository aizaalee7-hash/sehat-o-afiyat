How it works

  1. 1

    Post a request

    Share blood group, units, hospital and urgency. Hospital confirmation is always required.

  2. 2

    See transparent matches

    Donors are ranked by compatibility, city and area, availability, verification and urgency — with reasons shown.

  3. 3

    Donor responds

    Track every request: Pending, Matched, Request Sent, Accepted, Rejected, Completed, Cancelled.

  4. 4

    Hospital confirms

    The hospital or blood bank makes the final medical compatibility decision — never the app or AI.
